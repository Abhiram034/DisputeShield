# Evaluation

Synthetic scenario labels are stored as ground truth; they are not model outputs. Measure signal recall/precision, unsafe automatic-action rate, human-review rate, action success, customer complaint-after-action rate, and dispute incidence within observation windows. Report by scenario, merchant, amount tier and evidence sufficiency. Track false positives and false negatives separately; do not call an unobserved future dispute “prevented”. Compare intervention cohorts only with documented assignment methods and disclose confounding. Evaluate policy compliance as a hard constraint (target 100%), especially zero auto-contact/auto-refund for fraud-like cases and zero refund above configured limit. Low history and long-delay outcomes require delayed evaluation rather than immediate negative labels.
